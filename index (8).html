Model guidance & track assessment

  • Consensus: landfall window from Dauphin Island, AL to Destin, FL tonight. NHC's center line sits on the western Panhandle coast near the Santa Rosa/Okaloosa area.
  • West side: global models (Euro, GFS ensembles) have leaned west of NHC, toward Mobile Bay and Baldwin County.
  • East side: AI models (Google DeepMind) lean east, toward Destin/Fort Walton Beach, and tend to be faster and stronger. NHC leaned toward this side, and the storm was reported slightly east of the forecast track.
  • Fort Walton Beach: in the right-front (worst) quadrant under most scenarios. West outcomes put it about 50 miles east of center with surge and wind still severe. East outcomes put it near the core.
  • Intensity: regional models (HAFS, HWRF) and statistical guidance keep it near major-hurricane strength to landfall. Shear could weaken it, but a larger wind field makes impacts extend well east of center.
  • Confidence: track is fairly high within roughly 50 miles. The exact landfall point matters less than the surge and wind reaching the whole warned coast.
